jalen daniels

I’m a native New Yorker and former classical ballerina. I trained for 14+ years and have performed everywhere from the Apollo Theater to the Supreme Court and the White House. At Harvard, I honed my intellectual edge through a dual honors concentration in Social Studies and African American Studies. My summa award-winning thesis, “Enemies of The New: Transhumanism, Blackness, and Fleshless Imaginaries,” intervenes in the evolving conversation on race, humanity, and technology and is now moving toward publication.

After college, I worked at McKinsey & Co., focusing on entertainment, Web3/Crypto, and economic mobility; I ended my time working on the Firm’s first documentary film that premiered at Martha’s Vineyard Film Festival. I then served as Chief of Staff at an art and technology startup, which had partnerships with Sotheby’s, OpenAI, the Brooklyn Museum, Spotify, and more.

Today, my work sits at the intersection of culture, ideas, and institutional imagination. I serve as the youngest Trustee in the history of the Harvard Club of New York City, where I focus on revitalizing innovation and bringing world-class speakers with provoking ideas. I am grateful to be the Strategic Lead of an emerging private school in Houston ($65M raise) with a rich curriculum in Black Diasporic identity and theology. I have advised copious Fortune 500 companies, and I am a consultant on some of the most exciting brands, including some stealth startups started by ex TIME and GQ executives. In my free time, I host public speaking engagements on technology, theology, and philosophy.

bryan
cash

I’m a born-and-raised New Yorker and lifelong sports junkie—a kid from Jamaica, Queens who was obsessed with box scores, improbable correlations, and the energy of people. I’ve always seen the world through data long before it was a buzzword. I was the kid fascinated by how fast the NBA could aggregate Dwyane Wade’s performance based on Gabrielle Union’s attendance. That curiosity is what led me to study Computer Science at Syracuse. I wanted to help businesses uncover deep analytics that unlock real storytelling.

My career has always lived at the intersection of data and real-world impact. I audited multi-billion-dollar settlements, including the Volkswagen emissions scandal; rebuilt the analytics backbone for The Washington Post’s Publishing & Circulation team; and helped scale Vantage Risk’s Political, Risk & Credit unit from $70M to over $225M in Gross Written Premium (GWP), contributing to the firm surpassing $1B+ GWP. While compelled by the power of data across these experiences, I wanted to bring my technology journey full circle and build platforms with cultural resonance.

I created my own sports analytics company, delivering proprietary data you couldn’t find anywhere else. I then became CTO of an art and technology startup, which had partnerships with major institutions such as Sotheby’s, Spotify, OpenAI, the Brooklyn Museum, and more.

Today, my work blends engineering rigor with cultural intuition, and is always driven by the belief that technology should amplify our humanity, not replace it. That’s why we started Cyph.
